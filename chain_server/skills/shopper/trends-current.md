# Current Trend Snapshot - Summer/Fall 2026

This file gives the outfit-styling skill current seasonal context.
Not catalog truth: product names, prices, materials, colors, and availability
must come from catalog tools.

Use trends as light styling framing when they help the shopper decide. Do not
force trend language into simple product lookup, cart mutation, or budget-only
requests.

## How To Use This Snapshot

- Apply styling fundamentals first: occasion, proportion, color harmony,
  comfort, budget, and cart state.
- Use trend context as a tiebreaker when two grounded catalog options both work.
- Translate trends into intent when the exact item is not in the catalog.
- Do not repeat unsourced market claims, demand stats, runway claims, or
  absolutes to the shopper.
- Keep trend references short: one sentence is usually enough.

## Seasonal Direction

The current mood is soft romantic dressing balanced by structure: lace, satin,
fluid tailoring, elongated lines, and rich accent colors. For fall, texture and
layering become more important than bare summer ease.

Good shopper-facing phrasing:

- "This gives you the romantic-but-polished direction that feels current now."
- "The color keeps the outfit seasonal without making it feel too trendy."
- "This is a practical way to nod to the trend while staying within your budget."

Avoid:

- "This is the most in-demand item right now."
- "This color is universally flattering."
- "This is runway-approved."
- "Everyone is wearing this."

## Fabric And Texture

Natural textures such as linen, cotton twill, light denim, and washed silk are
useful seasonal signals when catalog details confirm them. Avoid describing a
product as natural, stiff, or synthetic-heavy without catalog evidence.

| Texture | Use It For | Pair With |
|---|---|---|
| Lace | Romantic focus, event dressing | Tailoring, simple knits, understated accessories |
| Satin | Day-to-night polish | Matte knit, structured jacket, clean shoe |
| Boucle | Fall occasion texture | Sleek shoe, minimal jewelry, simple bag |
| Sheer / chiffon / mesh | Light layering and movement | Structured base, opaque underlayer |
| Soft cotton / jersey | Practical summer base | One elevated piece so the outfit does not read too casual |
| Shearling / faux fur | Fall accent texture | Sleek base outfit, restrained color palette |

## Budget-Friendly Trend Translation

When budget is tight, do not chase expensive trend pieces. Use trend cues in
lower-cost or smaller-surface-area items.

| Trend Goal | Budget Translation |
|---|---|
| Current color | Shoe, bag, small accessory, print detail |
| Romantic texture | Lace-trim top, satin blouse, embroidered bag |
| Elongated line | Same-tone shoe and bottom, long skirt, vertical styling |
| Fluid tailoring | Polished knit, relaxed skirt, structured cardigan |
| Evening polish | Metallic, bead, embroidery, satin, or clutch accent |

## Shopper-Safe Notes

- Use "broadly wearable" instead of "universally flattering."
- Use "current" or "seasonal" instead of "everyone is wearing."
- Use "gives the same effect" when substituting for a missing trend item.
- If the catalog lacks a strong trend match, say so plainly and recommend the
  best grounded alternative.
- Never describe a catalog item as lace, satin, boucle, leather, wool, or silk
  unless that attribute came from catalog results or product details.
